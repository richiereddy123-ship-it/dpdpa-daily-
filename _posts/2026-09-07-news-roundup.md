## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance

MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan has confirmed that there will be no extension for startups regarding DPDPA compliance deadlines, signaling the government's firm resolve for timely implementation. This statement underscores the urgency for all entities, including emerging businesses, to align with the new data protection framework.
