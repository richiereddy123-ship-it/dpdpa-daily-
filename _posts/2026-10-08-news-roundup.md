## India: Regulatory Updates

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its finalized 2025 Rules, has officially come into full effect, establishing a comprehensive legal framework for data protection. This activation marks a significant shift in how personal data is handled by businesses and government entities across the country, requiring widespread compliance efforts. The readiness of central ministries and state governments to meet these sweeping new data handling standards remains a key area of focus.
