## India: DPDPA Takes Full Effect

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially come into full force, following the finalization and activation of the accompanying DPDP Rules in 2025. This landmark development establishes the comprehensive operational framework for data privacy enforcement across the nation, marking a pivotal moment for businesses and individuals alike. The DPDP Rules provide critical guidance on practical implementation, detailing aspects such as consent management, data fiduciary obligations, and the structure of the Data Protection Board.
