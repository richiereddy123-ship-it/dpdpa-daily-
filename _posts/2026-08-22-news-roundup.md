## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance
MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan reiterated that there would be no extensions for DPDPA compliance deadlines, urging startups and all data fiduciaries to accelerate their preparations. This firm stance underscores the government's commitment to timely enforcement, despite ongoing discussions about the readiness of various sectors.
