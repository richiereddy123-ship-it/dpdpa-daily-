## India: DPDPA Implementation and Compliance
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its 2025 Rules, has officially come into force, establishing a new era for data privacy in the country. This framework outlines significant obligations for data fiduciaries and strengthens the rights of data principals, with a strong emphasis on consent and data minimization principles.
