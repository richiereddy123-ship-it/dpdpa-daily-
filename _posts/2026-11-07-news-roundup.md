## India: DPDPA Takes Full Effect

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, along with its comprehensive 2025 Rules, has officially come into full force, ushering in a new era for data privacy regulation in India. This activation establishes the framework for data fiduciary and data principal obligations, alongside the operationalization of enforcement mechanisms. Businesses are now fully accountable to the new statutory requirements and the Data Protection Board's oversight.
