## India: DPDPA Implementation & Board Formation

MeitY has initiated the process of staffing the Data Protection Board of India by inviting applications for its Chairperson and members, a critical step towards operationalizing the DPDPA's enforcement mechanism. This move signifies the government's commitment to establishing the regulatory oversight mandated by the new privacy law. Meanwhile, the DPDP Rules 2025 are being discussed, aiming to provide practical guidance for data fiduciaries on compliance. The government also recognized IDfy in its DPDP Innovation Challenge, encouraging technological solutions for privacy enforcement.
