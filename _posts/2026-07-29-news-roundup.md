## India: DPDPA Officially in Force
The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its 2025 Rules, has officially been brought into force, marking a significant milestone for India's data privacy landscape. This activation ushers in a new era of data protection, establishing a comprehensive legal framework for handling personal data across the country and requiring swift action from data fiduciaries.
