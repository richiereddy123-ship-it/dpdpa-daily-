## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, along with its finalized 2025 rules, has officially come into force, marking a pivotal moment for data privacy in the country. This new framework introduces significant compliance obligations for all entities processing personal data.
