## India: DPDPA Takes Full Effect

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, has officially come into force, following the finalization and activation of its accompanying Rules in 2025. This marks a significant milestone in the nation's data privacy landscape, providing the operational framework for the Act's implementation and enforcement. The comprehensive rules clarify various aspects, from consent mechanisms to data breach notifications. ## India: Compliance and Operational Shifts

Businesses across India are now intensely focused on understanding and implementing the extensive compliance requirements outlined in the DPDPA and its Rules. Experts are detailing the top operational impacts, which include revisiting data mapping, updating consent management systems, and establishing robust grievance redressal mechanisms to align with the new legal mandates.
