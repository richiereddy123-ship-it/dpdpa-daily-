## India: DPDPA Takes Full Effect

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act of 2023 has officially come into full effect, with the accompanying DPDP Rules 2025 now finalized and operational. This marks a pivotal moment for data privacy enforcement across the nation, establishing a comprehensive legal framework for personal data processing. The activation of these rules provides essential guidance for the implementation of the Act, revolutionizing India's data protection landscape.
