## India: DPDPA Implementation & Litigation

The Supreme Court is currently examining multiple Public Interest Litigations (PILs) challenging the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and has sought the Centre's response. This ongoing legal scrutiny raises fundamental questions about the Act's foundational validity even as its compliance deadlines loom.
