## India: Regulatory Updates

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), along with its accompanying 2025 Rules, has officially come into force, marking a pivotal moment for data privacy in the country. This activation establishes a robust framework for personal data protection and initiates a new era of compliance obligations for data fiduciaries across all sectors. The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has also initiated the process for operationalizing the Data Protection Board by inviting applications for its Chairperson and members, signaling the imminent establishment of the key enforcement body.
