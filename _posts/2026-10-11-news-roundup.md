## India: Regulatory Updates

IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed the finalization of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules, signaling their imminent publication and a significant step towards the full operationalization of India's new data protection framework. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan further emphasized that no extensions would be granted for DPDP Act implementation, urging startups and other entities to prepare for the upcoming compliance deadline. This firm stance underscores the government's commitment to timely adoption of the new data protection standards, even as discussions highlight the extensive compliance challenges faced by ministries and state governments.
