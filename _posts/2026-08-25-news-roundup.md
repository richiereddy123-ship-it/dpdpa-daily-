## India: Regulatory Updates and Compliance Landscape

Union IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced that the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules have been finalized and are slated for publication in the coming days, marking a crucial step towards the Act's full implementation. This development signals that data fiduciaries should prepare for imminent compliance deadlines, with MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan reiterating that no extensions are expected, particularly for startups. The Act's framework, including the role of consent managers and potential director liability for non-compliance, continues to be a key focus for businesses.
