## India: DPDPA Implementation & Challenges

MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan confirmed no further extensions for DPDPA implementation, urging startups to meet compliance deadlines and underscoring the government's commitment to timely enforcement. Separately, MeitY informed Parliament that Common Service Centre (CSC) operators are prohibited from storing Aadhaar or banking data, reinforcing data minimization principles for sensitive personal information.
