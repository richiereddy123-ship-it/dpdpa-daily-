## India: DPDPA Implementation and Challenges

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and its accompanying 2025 Rules are now fully operational, fundamentally reshaping data privacy obligations across the country. Businesses are actively seeking guidance on compliance, with analyses highlighting operational impacts, cross-border data transfer implications, and specific sector considerations like banking. The "DPDP (Removal of Difficulties) Order 2026" was recently issued, providing crucial clarifications on consent and data audit requirements.
