## India: Regulatory Updates & Compliance
The Indian government has issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026, providing crucial clarifications on consent and data audit requirements under the DPDPA. This order aims to streamline compliance for Data Fiduciaries as the law fully comes into effect. Concurrently, IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed that the DPDPA Rules, 2025 have been finalized and are slated for imminent publication, marking a significant step towards full operationalization of the Act.
