## India: Regulatory & Enforcement Developments

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the process of forming the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) by inviting applications for its chairperson and members. This move signals a critical step towards the full operationalization and enforcement of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). MeitY also recognized IDfy in its DPDP Innovation Challenge, highlighting government efforts to foster technological solutions for privacy enforcement.
