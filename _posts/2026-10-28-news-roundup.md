## India: DPDPA Takes Full Effect

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially come into full effect with the finalization and activation of the DPDP Rules 2025. This landmark development ushers in a new era of data privacy compliance for all entities operating within or targeting the Indian market, requiring immediate attention to updated data processing practices. Various industry and legal experts are actively disseminating guidance on the operational impacts, emphasizing the need for robust data governance frameworks to ensure timely adherence.
