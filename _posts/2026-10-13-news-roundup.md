## India: Regulatory & Legislative Progress

**MeitY Invites Applications for Data Protection Board**
The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially opened applications for the Chairperson and members of the Data Protection Board of India. This marks a critical step towards operationalizing the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), establishing the adjudicatory body responsible for enforcing the new privacy law. The Board's formation is eagerly awaited by businesses and data fiduciaries preparing for compliance.
