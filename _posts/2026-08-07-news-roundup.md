## India: Regulatory Updates & Board Operationalisation

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the process to operationalize the Data Protection Board of India, inviting applications for its Chairperson and members. This marks a critical step towards establishing the enforcement body for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.
