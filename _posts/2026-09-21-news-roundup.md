## India: Regulatory Updates & DPDPA Implementation

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially initiated the process to staff the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI), inviting applications for its chairperson and members. This critical move paves the way for the operationalization of the DPDPA's independent enforcement body.
