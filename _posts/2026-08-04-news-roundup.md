## India: DPDPA Implementation & Data Protection Board

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially commenced the process of operationalizing India's Data Protection Board by inviting applications for its Chairperson and other members. This crucial step signals the government's intent to establish the independent regulatory body responsible for enforcing the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023. This development is key to moving from legislation to actual governance of data privacy in India.
