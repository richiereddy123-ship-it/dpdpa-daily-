## India: Regulatory Updates & Compliance Milestones

**Supreme Court to Hear DPDPA Challenges**
India's Supreme Court is preparing to hear petitions challenging the constitutional validity of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This significant development indicates ongoing legal scrutiny over the foundational privacy law, even as its phased implementation progresses. The outcome could shape future interpretations and enforcement of the DPDPA.
