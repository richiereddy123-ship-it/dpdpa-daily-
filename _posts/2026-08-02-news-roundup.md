## India: Regulatory & Compliance Updates

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the crucial process of operationalizing the Data Protection Board, inviting applications for its Chairperson and members. This marks a significant step towards the full implementation and enforcement of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
