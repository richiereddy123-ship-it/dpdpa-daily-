## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, is now fully in force, accompanied by the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, which provide detailed guidance for its implementation. Businesses are actively reviewing these rules to understand their compliance obligations, with numerous guides emerging to navigate the operational impacts across various sectors like Telecom, Media & Technology (TMT) and banking. The new regime marks a significant shift in India's data privacy landscape, emphasizing accountability for Data Fiduciaries.

## India: Regulatory Clarifications & Legal Challenges

The Centre has issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026, aiming to clarify specific provisions related to consent mechanisms and audit requirements under the DPDPA. This order seeks to streamline the implementation process for Data Fiduciaries. Simultaneously, the constitutionality of the DPDPA, 2023, has been challenged before the Supreme Court, indicating ongoing legal scrutiny of the landmark privacy legislation.
