The rapid expansion of the edtech sector globally has brought the privacy of student data into sharp focus. For Indian edtech companies operating domestically and eyeing international markets, understanding the nuances of data protection laws is critical. This analysis anchors on India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, comparing it against the U.S. Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) to highlight key differences and commonalities in safeguarding student information.
