## India: Regulatory Updates and Compliance Focus

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially commenced the process of operationalizing the Data Protection Board, inviting applications for its chairperson and members. This critical step signifies the imminent full enforcement of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, and the establishment of India's primary data protection authority.
