## India: Regulatory Updates & Board Operationalization

MeitY has formally begun the process to operationalize the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI), inviting applications for its chairperson and members. This critical step will establish the independent adjudicatory body responsible for enforcing the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and its accompanying rules. Furthermore, a senior MeitY official indicated that the ministry is actively seeking industry feedback on proposals to potentially shorten compliance timelines for data protection, signaling a proactive approach to DPDPA implementation.
