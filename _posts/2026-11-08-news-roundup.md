## India: DPDPA Takes Full Effect with Finalized Rules

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, has officially come into full force with the finalization and activation of its accompanying rules in 2025. This marks a significant shift in India's data privacy landscape, introducing comprehensive obligations for data fiduciaries and rights for data principals. The operationalization of these rules provides much-anticipated clarity on compliance requirements, including consent mechanisms, data breach notifications, and the structure of the Data Protection Board.
