## India: DPDPA Implementation & Governance

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, along with the recently notified DPDP Rules 2025, continues to be a central focus for Indian businesses. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan reiterated that there would be no extension to the compliance deadline, urging startups and other entities to finalize their preparations for the comprehensive framework governing personal data processing. Meanwhile, the Supreme Court has sought the Centre's response to Public Interest Litigations challenging various provisions of the DPDPA.
