## India: DPDPA Implementation and Clarifications

The Central government has issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026, providing crucial clarifications on consent mechanisms and data audit requirements under the DPDPA. This order aims to streamline compliance efforts and address initial ambiguities faced by data fiduciaries.
