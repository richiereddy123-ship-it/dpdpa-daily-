## India: Regulatory and Legal Developments

The Supreme Court has sought the Centre's response regarding Public Interest Litigations challenging the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This crucial development coincides with MeitY initiating the process of appointing the chairperson and members for the Data Protection Board of India, signaling progress towards the Act's full operationalization amidst ongoing legal scrutiny.
