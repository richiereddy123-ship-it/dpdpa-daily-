## India: Data Protection Board & DPDPA Operationalization

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially commenced the process of constituting the Data Protection Board of India, inviting applications for its Chairperson and members. This crucial step signals the imminent operationalization of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, moving India closer to full privacy enforcement. Separately, an innovation challenge by MeitY and NeGD saw IDfy recognized for its contributions to DPDP solutions, highlighting governmental efforts to foster privacy-enhancing technologies.

## India: DPDPA Compliance Readiness & Sectoral Impact

With critical compliance deadlines for the DPDPA approaching in 2026-27, Indian enterprises, including MSMEs and Global Capability Centres (GCCs), are urged to prioritize data security at the source and implement robust compliance frameworks. The Act's comprehensive requirements necessitate a deep understanding of consent management, data fiduciary obligations, and the forthcoming DPDP Rules 2025. Experts emphasize that compliance is not exclusive to large organizations, with tailored strategies needed for smaller businesses.

## Cross-Border Considerations

The DPDPA introduces new complexities for cross-border data transfers, particularly for sensitive data like healthcare information, requiring Indian entities to align their practices with global standards such as the GDPR. The ongoing discussions highlight the need for a harmonized approach to data protection that facilitates international data flows while upholding individual privacy rights.
([Source](https://www.livelaw.in/articles/cross-border-transfer-of-healthcare-data-reconciling-indias-dpdpa-with-gdpr-260388))

Why it matters: The active steps towards operationalizing India's Data Protection Board underscore the nation's commitment to a robust privacy framework, demanding urgent compliance readiness from businesses and signaling India's growing influence in the global data protection landscape.
