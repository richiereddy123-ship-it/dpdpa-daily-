## India: Regulatory & Compliance Updates

IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed that the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) rules have been finalized and are set for publication in the coming days. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan subsequently clarified that no extension would be granted for startups, urging all entities to prepare for the strict compliance deadlines and potential liabilities for directors under the new law.
