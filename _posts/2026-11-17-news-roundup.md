## India: DPDPA Implementation and Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, has officially come into force following the finalization and activation of the DPDP Rules, 2025. These rules provide crucial operational guidance, detailing the compliance obligations for data fiduciaries and processors across various sectors. Businesses are now intensively navigating the practical implications, focusing on consent frameworks, data principal rights, breach notification protocols, and the forthcoming Data Protection Board's role.

## India: Sector-Specific Impacts and Legal Challenges

The DPDPA's implementation introduces significant operational shifts for various sectors, including banking, telecom, media, and technology, necessitating comprehensive overhauls of existing data governance frameworks. Concerns have also emerged regarding the Act's potential to complicate journalistic practices and impose new business challenges for media organizations. Furthermore, the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, is reportedly facing scrutiny, signaling potential legal battles ahead for this landmark privacy legislation.

## India: Cross-Border Data Transfers and AI Governance

The DPDP Rules 2025 establish a new framework for cross-border data transfers, potentially adopting a "whitelisting" or "blacklisting" approach that will significantly impact global businesses. This development also highlights an "adequacy dilemma" for India, as its data transfer mechanisms will be closely compared with international standards like the GDPR. In a related development, India has also issued AI governance guidelines for the Asia-Pacific region, underscoring its proactive stance on regulating emerging technologies alongside data privacy.
