## India: DPDPA Implementation and Compliance

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has reiterated that there will be no extensions for compliance deadlines under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), urging startups and businesses to prepare for imminent enforcement. This firm stance follows a push from the Cabinet Secretary to ensure all government ministries and state bodies are also on track with their data protection readiness, highlighting the widespread impact of the new law. Experts continue to analyze the critical compliance timelines for 2026-27, emphasizing the need for robust data governance frameworks to avoid potential penalties and director exposure.
