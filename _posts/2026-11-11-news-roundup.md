## India: DPDPA Enactment and Compliance

India's landmark Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially been brought into full force with the notification of the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. This significant development provides the much-anticipated operational framework for the Act, detailing compliance obligations for data fiduciaries and the rights of data principals.
