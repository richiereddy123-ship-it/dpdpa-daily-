## India: Regulatory Milestones & Compliance

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) has officially taken full effect, with IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirming the finalization and publication of the accompanying rules. This marks a pivotal moment for India's data privacy landscape, establishing a comprehensive legal framework for data processing.
