## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance

MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan confirmed that there would be no extension to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) compliance deadline, urging startups and other entities to prepare for timely implementation. This firm stance underscores the government's commitment to operationalizing the new data protection regime.
