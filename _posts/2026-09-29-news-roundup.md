## India: Regulatory & Enforcement Foundation

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially commenced the process of operationalizing India's Data Protection Board, inviting applications for its Chairperson and members. This crucial step signals the imminent establishment of the independent body responsible for enforcing the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan reiterated that no extensions will be granted for DPDPA compliance deadlines, emphasizing that startups and all entities must be prepared for the upcoming enforcement timeline.
