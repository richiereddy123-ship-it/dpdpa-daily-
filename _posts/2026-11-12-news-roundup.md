## India: DPDPA Enters Force

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, has officially come into force following the finalization and release of the accompanying DPDP Rules, 2025. This marks a pivotal moment for data privacy in India, establishing a comprehensive legal framework for the processing of digital personal data. Businesses are now urged to rapidly align their operations with the new compliance requirements outlined in the Act and Rules.
