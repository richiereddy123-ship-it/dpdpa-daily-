## India: DPDPA Implementation & Regulatory Updates

MeitY is reportedly considering a compressed compliance timeline for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and intends to soon notify cross-border data transfer restrictions for Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs). MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan emphasized that no extensions to the compliance deadlines are anticipated, urging all businesses, including startups, to accelerate their preparations.
