## India: Regulatory Updates

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the application process for members of the Data Protection Board, a critical step towards fully establishing the DPDPA's enforcement arm. This move addresses concerns regarding the Board's vacancy and its potential impact on upcoming compliance deadlines, including those slated for May 2027. MeitY is also actively seeking industry input on potentially accelerating the DPDPA compliance timelines, indicating a flexible approach to the implementation schedule.
