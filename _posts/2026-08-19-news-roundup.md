## India: Regulatory Updates and Litigation

The Supreme Court of India has formally requested a response from the Centre regarding several Public Interest Litigations (PILs) challenging the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This development underscores ongoing legal scrutiny of the newly implemented law, with petitioners raising concerns about its provisions.
