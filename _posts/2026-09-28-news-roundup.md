## India: DPDPA Implementation & Challenges

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its 2025 Rules, has officially come into full effect, establishing a comprehensive framework for data privacy across the nation. This activation marks a significant shift in how personal data is handled by entities operating within India.
