## India: Regulatory & Implementation Updates

IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed that the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules 2025 have been finalized and are slated for imminent publication. This marks a critical step towards full operationalization of the DPDPA, providing much-anticipated clarity on various provisions. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan reiterated that there will be no extension to the DPDPA compliance deadlines, urging startups and other entities to prioritize readiness.
