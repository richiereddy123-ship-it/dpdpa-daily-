## India: DPDPA Nears Full Implementation

Union IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced that the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act rules have been finalized and are slated for imminent publication. This marks a critical step towards the full operationalization of India's landmark privacy law, providing much-anticipated clarity on compliance requirements.
