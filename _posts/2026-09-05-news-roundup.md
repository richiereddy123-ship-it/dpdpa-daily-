## India: Regulatory Updates & Compliance

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) is actively seeking industry feedback on potentially shortening the compliance timelines for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). This move suggests a desire to accelerate the implementation phase, which could present new challenges and opportunities for businesses. Separately, MeitY has reiterated that Common Service Centre (CSC) operators are strictly prohibited from storing sensitive Aadhaar or banking data, reinforcing data security protocols for critical citizen services.
