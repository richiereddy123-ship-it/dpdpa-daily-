## India: Regulatory Updates and Compliance
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its associated Rules of 2025, has officially come into force, establishing a comprehensive data protection framework. This landmark legislation introduces robust obligations for data fiduciaries and grants enhanced rights to data principals, fundamentally reshaping how personal data is handled across all sectors. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan confirmed there will be no extension to compliance deadlines, urging startups and all entities to prepare.
