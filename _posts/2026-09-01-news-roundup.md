## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) continues to be a central focus, with the MeitY Secretary affirming that no extensions will be granted for compliance deadlines, urging startups and other entities to prepare. Government ministries and state bodies are also undergoing significant readiness assessments to ensure their own compliance with the sweeping new data protection framework.
