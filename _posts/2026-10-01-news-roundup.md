## India: Regulatory Updates and Implementation
The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has confirmed that there will be no extension for compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), urging startups and other entities to adhere to the established deadlines. This firm stance underscores the government's commitment to the timely implementation of India's new privacy framework. Concurrently, MeitY has initiated the process of operationalizing the Data Protection Board of India by inviting applications for its Chairperson and members, marking a crucial step towards establishing the DPDPA's enforcement authority.
