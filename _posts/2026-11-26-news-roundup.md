## India: DPDPA Operationalization and Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its accompanying 2025 Rules, is now fully operational, marking a significant shift in the nation's data privacy landscape. Organizations are actively navigating the phased commencement and core obligations, developing robust compliance strategies to meet the new requirements. Key operational impacts include updated data processing practices, consent management frameworks, and the establishment of internal governance structures.
