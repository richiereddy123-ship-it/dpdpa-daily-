## India: DPDPA Implementation and Regulatory Action

MeitY has officially initiated the process to operationalize India's Data Protection Board, inviting applications for the Chairperson and members. This move is a critical step towards establishing the enforcement body for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), signaling that regulatory oversight is rapidly taking shape.
