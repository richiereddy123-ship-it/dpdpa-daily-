## India: Regulatory Updates and Compliance
The Supreme Court has requested a response from the Centre regarding Public Interest Litigations (PILs) challenging the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). This development underscores ongoing legal scrutiny of the landmark privacy law. Meanwhile, MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan confirmed that there would be no extension for the DPDPA compliance deadline, urging startups and all data fiduciaries to ensure readiness for the new regulations, including the recently released DPDP Rules 2025.
