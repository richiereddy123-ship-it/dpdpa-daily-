## India: DPDPA Implementation and Challenges

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially come into force with the finalization of its accompanying rules in 2025, marking a significant shift in India's data privacy landscape. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan has affirmed there will be no extension for compliance deadlines, emphasizing that even startups must adhere to the new regulations promptly. The government is also engaging the public, having launched a contest for a DPDPA logo and tagline.
