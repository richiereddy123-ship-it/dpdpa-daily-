## India: DPDPA Implementation and Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its 2025 Rules, has officially come into force, marking a significant shift in the nation's data privacy landscape. This activation ushers in a new era of enforcement and compliance requirements for data fiduciaries across all sectors.
