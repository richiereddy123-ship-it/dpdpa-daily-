## India: DPDPA Activation and Compliance

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, is now fully operational following the finalization and activation of its accompanying 2025 Rules, marking a significant shift in India's data privacy landscape. This activation has prompted a surge in guidance from legal and consulting firms on the fundamentals of compliance for data fiduciaries and data principals. Businesses are actively navigating the new framework, which introduces comprehensive obligations for handling personal data.
