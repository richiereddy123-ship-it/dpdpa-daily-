## India: DPDPA Implementation and Regulatory Developments

*   **DPDPA Enters Force with 2025 Rules:** India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially been brought into force, accompanied by the finalization of its 2025 Rules. This activation marks a significant shift in India's data privacy landscape, requiring data fiduciaries across all sectors, including government ministries and state bodies, to rapidly assess and implement compliance measures. Experts emphasize that the new framework, while providing clarity, also presents challenges for startups and established entities alike in understanding its fundamental requirements.
