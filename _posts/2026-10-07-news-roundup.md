## India: DPDPA Takes Effect & Compliance Focus

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its 2025 Rules, has officially come into force, establishing a comprehensive legal framework for personal data protection across the nation. This activation marks a significant shift, requiring all entities handling personal data to align with the new regulations.
