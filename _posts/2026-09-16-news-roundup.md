## India: DPDPA Enters Force & Implementation

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025, has officially come into force, marking a pivotal moment in the nation's data privacy landscape. This activation ushers in a new era of compliance requirements for data fiduciaries and data principals across various sectors. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan has confirmed that no extensions will be granted for startups, emphasizing the immediate need for compliance.
