## India: DPDPA Takes Effect

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially come into force, following the finalization and notification of the accompanying Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025. This landmark legislation, now fully activated, ushers in a new era of data privacy enforcement and compliance obligations for entities operating within India. Businesses are now urged to rapidly implement robust data protection frameworks to align with the new legal requirements.
