## India: DPDPA & Regulatory Landscape

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, remains a focal point for Indian businesses, establishing a common framework for personal data protection nationwide and driving discussions on its foundational principles. With the DPDP Rules 2025 anticipated, companies are preparing to integrate these granular guidelines, which are crucial for clarifying consent mechanisms and data breach notification procedures. The IAPP has also released resources to help data protection professionals understand their new responsibilities under the Act.
