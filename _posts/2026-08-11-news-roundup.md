## India: Regulatory & Judicial Developments

The Supreme Court is actively addressing Public Interest Litigations (PILs) challenging the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, having requested the Centre's response. This indicates ongoing judicial scrutiny of the new law's foundational aspects. The Court also directed MeitY to investigate a PIL concerning the recovery or destruction of Indian citizens' personal data stolen and stored on foreign servers, highlighting judicial concern with data breach remediation and cross-border data governance. Separately, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the formation of India's Data Protection Board by inviting applications for its members, marking a critical step towards operationalizing the DPDPA's enforcement mechanism.
