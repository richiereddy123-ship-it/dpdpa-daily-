## India: DPDPA Implementation & Challenges

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, along with its finalized 2025 Rules, is now fully operational, ushering in a new era of data privacy enforcement. Businesses are actively engaging with comprehensive compliance guides to navigate the practical implementation of the law's provisions, which detail obligations for data fiduciaries and rights for data principals.
