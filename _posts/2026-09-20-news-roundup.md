## India: Regulatory & Implementation Milestones

*   **Data Protection Board Formation Commences:** The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has officially invited applications for the Chairperson and members of the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI). This marks a crucial step towards establishing the regulatory authority responsible for enforcing the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
