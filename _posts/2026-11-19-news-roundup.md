## India: DPDPA Implementation and Challenges

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, along with its comprehensive DPDP Rules 2025, is now fully operational, fundamentally reshaping India's data privacy landscape. The finalization of these rules, particularly those governing consent management, provides crucial clarity for data fiduciaries on compliance obligations. This marks a pivotal moment for data protection enforcement in the country.
