## India: DPDPA Implementation and Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 has officially been brought into force, following the finalization and activation of its accompanying 2025 Rules. This landmark legislation now provides the operational framework for data protection across the country, marking a significant shift in India's privacy landscape. Businesses are actively navigating the DPDPA's operational impacts, with various guides emerging to help understand compliance fundamentals, including data principal rights, consent management, and data fiduciary obligations.
