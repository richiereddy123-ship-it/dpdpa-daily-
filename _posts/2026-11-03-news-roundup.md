## India: DPDPA Implementation and Judicial Scrutiny

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) of 2023, along with its finalized 2025 rules, has officially come into force, marking a significant shift in the nation's data privacy landscape. This activation has prompted a wave of compliance efforts across various sectors, including banking. However, the Act's constitutionality is already facing challenges in the Supreme Court, particularly from media associations concerned about its potential impact on press freedom and newsgathering.
