## India: Regulatory Milestones

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the process for operationalizing India's Data Protection Board, inviting applications for its Chairperson and members. This marks a crucial step towards establishing the enforcement and adjudication body under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023. The Board's formation is eagerly awaited as it will be central to resolving disputes and imposing penalties for data protection violations.
