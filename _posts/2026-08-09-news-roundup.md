## India: DPDPA & Regulatory Developments

The Supreme Court has formally sought a response from the Centre regarding Public Interest Litigations (PILs) challenging the constitutionality of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). This development underscores ongoing legal scrutiny of the landmark privacy legislation. Concurrently, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the operationalisation of the Data Protection Board of India, inviting applications for its Chairperson and members, marking a crucial step towards the Act's enforcement.

## DPDPA Compliance & Sectoral Impact

Indian enterprises are urged to secure data at the source and navigate DPDPA compliance, with specific guidance emerging for MSMEs to manage requirements without extensive IT teams. The Act's implications are being felt across various sectors, from e-wallet architecture facing data localization demands to the connected healthcare and Med-Tech industries adapting to new privacy standards. WhatsApp has also begun testing a date-of-birth feature in India, anticipating the DPDPA's age-verification rules for processing minors' data.
