## India: Regulatory & Compliance Updates

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the crucial process of establishing the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) by inviting applications for its chairperson and members. This significant step brings the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) closer to full operationalization, signaling a new era for data governance in the country. Concurrently, MeitY is actively seeking industry feedback on proposals to accelerate the compliance timeline for the DPDPA, which could significantly impact businesses needing to adjust their data processing practices sooner than initially expected.
