## India: Regulatory Updates
The Indian government has issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026, aiming to clarify provisions related to consent mechanisms and data audit requirements under the DPDPA. This move seeks to provide much-needed guidance for data fiduciaries in their compliance efforts.
