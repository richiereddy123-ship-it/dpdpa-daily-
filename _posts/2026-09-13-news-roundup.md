## India: Regulatory Milestones & Compliance Focus

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the critical process of operationalizing the Data Protection Board (DPB) by inviting applications for its Chairperson and members. This marks a significant step towards establishing the enforcement body for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
