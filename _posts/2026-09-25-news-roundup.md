## India: Regulatory Updates & DPDPA Implementation

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has initiated the process to staff the Data Protection Board of India, inviting applications for the chairperson and other members. This crucial step signals the imminent operationalization of the enforcement body for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).
