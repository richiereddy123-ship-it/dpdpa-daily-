## India: DPDPA Implementation & Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 and the accompanying DPDP Rules 2025 are now fully in force, ushering in a new era for data privacy. Businesses are actively developing "Board-ready" compliance strategies, focusing on core obligations like consent management, data principal rights, and robust grievance redressal mechanisms. Specific guidance is emerging for sectors such as banking and Telecom, Media & Technology (TMT), detailing the operational impacts and tailored compliance requirements for these industries.
