## India: Regulatory & Implementation
The government has issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order 2026, providing crucial clarifications on consent mechanisms and data audit requirements under the DPDP Act. This initiative aims to ease the implementation process and address initial ambiguities for data fiduciaries. Union IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed that the DPDP Rules have been finalized and are set for imminent publication, marking a significant step towards the Act's full operationalization. MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan further emphasized that no extensions will be granted for DPDP Act compliance, urging startups and other entities to ensure timely readiness. Meanwhile, government ministries and state bodies are actively assessing their own data compliance, facing a sweeping test to adhere to the new stringent privacy norms.
