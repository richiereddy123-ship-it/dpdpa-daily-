## India: DPDPA Activation and Rules
The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, is now fully active, with the accompanying 2025 Rules providing essential clarity on its implementation. These rules, finalized by MeitY, offer detailed guidance, especially concerning robust consent management frameworks required for Data Fiduciaries and the establishment of the Data Protection Board.
