## India: DPDPA & Regulatory Updates

The Supreme Court has issued notices to the Centre concerning multiple petitions filed by media organizations challenging the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA). These pleas contend that certain provisions of the Act could hinder press freedom and journalistic operations by imposing stringent data processing obligations. While the government has expressed openness to media suggestions, it aims to balance privacy protection with press liberty.
