## India: DPDPA Implementation & Challenges

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) issued a "Removal of Difficulties" Order in 2026, clarifying certain aspects of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), particularly regarding consent mechanisms and data audit requirements for Data Fiduciaries. This move aims to provide clearer guidance for businesses beginning their compliance journey. IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed that the DPDPA Rules have been finalized and are slated for imminent publication, marking a significant step towards the Act's full operationalization.
