## India: Regulatory Updates and Compliance Deadlines

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026. This order aims to provide crucial clarifications regarding consent mechanisms and data audit requirements under the DPDPA, offering much-needed guidance for organizations navigating the new law. Meanwhile, IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw confirmed that the final DPDPA rules are complete and expected to be published shortly.
